A macOS menu bar app that puts a movable lens over your screen and shows what's
beneath it the way it may look to someone with a color vision deficiency.

Drag the lens over a simulator, a browser or a design tool and compare. The app
underneath needs no integration, and clicks inside the lens go straight through
to it. Switch between eight simulations, from deuteranomaly to achromatopsia,
tune the severity, or hold a shortcut to flash back to the original colors.

The cone-related simulations use the published Machado (2009) model; the
achromatic modes are labeled approximations. Capture stays on your Mac: frames
are processed in memory and never saved or uploaded.

**Features:**

- Eight color vision simulations plus original colors
- Click-through lens you can move and resize over any app
- Severity and blend-with-original sliders, plus hold-to-compare
- Configurable global keyboard shortcuts
- Works across Spaces and over full-screen apps
- In-app guide to color vision deficiency and designing for it
